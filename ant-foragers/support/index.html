SupportAnt Foragers

Ant Foragers is an ant-colony logic puzzle game for iPhone by exception games.

Contact

Write to exceptiongames@icloud.com. We read every message and answer in English, Ukrainian or Russian.

Reporting a bug? Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, the workshop number and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Questions and answers

Where is my progress saved?
Only on your iPhone. The game has no accounts and no cloud sync. Deleting the app erases all progress, so update the app without deleting it.
How do I start a workshop over?
Tap Restart. To replay the tutorial, open Settings → Replay tutorial.
I’m stuck. What can I do?
Tap Undo to take back the last signal, or tap Hint to see the next signal. A workshop solved with a hint earns one star; tap Restart and solve it on your own for more. The workshop of the day has no hint.
What are the working ants?
From workshop 6 on, starting a new workshop sends out one of five working ants, shared by everyone on the iPhone. Undo, Restart and leaving the workshop never cost another ant, and a started workshop always stays playable. Solve it with ★★★ without a hint and the ant comes home; after any other win it rests, and resting ants come back one every 10 minutes. Workshops 1–5, replays and the workshop of the day are free. When no ants are left, Free refill in Working ants brings all five back once a day, at least eight hours after the previous refill. With the Anthill, working ants never run out.
Can several people play on one iPhone?
Yes. Add players in Settings → Players. Each player has their own stars, records and ants. The working ants and the purchases are shared by everyone on the iPhone.
How do I keep progress safe or move it to another iPhone?
Ask a grown-up to open Settings → Backup copy → Save a copy and keep the file in Files, for example in iCloud Drive. On the other iPhone, or if the game ever starts from scratch, use Restore from a copy. Restoring replaces the progress on that iPhone. If a grown-up picked the wrong file, Bring back the previous progress in the same place returns what was there before. Purchases are not in the copy: they belong to the Apple Account (see below).
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
There are no ads. There are three optional purchases for grown-ups, each paid once and kept for good: the Anthill (working ants never run out), the Secret map: chapters 1–5 (the hints of every secret in chapters 1–5 for every player), and Anthill and Secret map together. They are only in Settings → Purchases for grown-ups, behind a parental gate; a child never sees a price or an offer. Apple handles the payment. The puzzles stay free, and ants in the shop are bought only with stars earned in the game.
How do I restore purchases?
Purchases belong to the Apple Account that made them, not to the game’s progress, so a backup copy does not carry them. They usually come back by themselves. If one is missing, for example on a new iPhone, a grown-up opens Settings → Purchases for grown-ups and taps Restore purchases. Prices, buying and restoring need the internet; everything else works offline.
Does Family Sharing work?
Yes. If your family shares purchases in Family Sharing, a purchase made by one family member works on the other family members’ iPhones too. If it does not appear, tap Restore purchases.
A purchase says “Waiting for a parent’s approval.” What now?
Ask to Buy sent the request to a parent in your family group. Nothing is unlocked until they approve it; then the purchase appears by itself. Apple does not tell the game when a request is declined, so the note goes away on its own within two days. To send a new request, a grown-up can tap Try again.
It says “Purchases are turned off on this phone (Screen Time).” What does that mean?
Screen Time on this iPhone does not allow in-app purchases. A parent can allow them in the iPhone’s Screen Time settings or leave them off: the game keeps working, and Free refill still works once a day.
Does the game collect data?
No. There are no analytics, accounts or tracking. Apple handles purchases, and we never see payment details. See the Privacy Policy.
Which devices are supported?
iPhone with iOS 18 or later. The game works without the internet; only prices, buying and restoring purchases need a connection.
