Ant Foragers

An ant-colony logic puzzle for iPhone by exception games.

Send signals along rows and columns, move across soil and leaves, and lead every ant to its nest. More than sixty handcrafted workshops, a new workshop of the day every day, and sixty secrets hidden among them.

Made for the whole family: a hint for when you are stuck, a step-by-step introduction to every new tile, instructions read aloud for players who cannot read yet, and several players on one iPhone.

No ads, no accounts and no timer on the puzzles. From workshop 6 on, each new workshop sends out one of five working ants, and they come back on their own; replays and the workshop of the day are free. Optional purchases for grown-ups wait behind a parental gate. No internet needed to play.

iPhone · iOS 18 or later · 43 languages
