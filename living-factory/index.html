Living Factory

An ant-colony logic puzzle for iPhone by exception games.

Send signals along rows and columns, move across soil and leaves, and lead every ant to its nest. Fifty handcrafted workshops in four chapters, a new workshop of the day every day, and a secret hidden in every workshop.

Made for the whole family: a hint for when you are stuck, a step-by-step introduction to every new tile, instructions read aloud for children who cannot read yet, and several players on one iPhone.

No ads, no purchases for real money, no accounts, no timer. No internet needed.
