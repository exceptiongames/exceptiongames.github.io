Privacy PolicyLiving Factory

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Living Factory (“the app”) is an ant-colony logic puzzle game for iPhone. exception games is the trade name of its developer, the person or company shown as the seller on the app’s App Store page, who is responsible for this policy (“we”). This policy explains what happens to information in the app, on this website and in messages to support.

In short: the app collects no data. Everything it saves stays on your iPhone, unless a grown-up saves a backup copy somewhere else.

Data the app collects

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It contains no advertising, analytics, accounts, tracking or third-party SDKs, and it does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (current workshop, unfinished games with undo history, completed workshops, best results and stars, results of the Workshop of the day, which workshops were solved with a hint, ants bought with stars or won for a perfect chapter, found secrets and how far their hints have opened, tutorial status), the names of players added on this device, and the sound, haptics and read-aloud settings are stored locally on your device using Apple’s standard app storage.

Player names are optional; you can keep the default “Player 2” and so on. The app never sends this data anywhere. It may be included in your own device backups under Apple’s terms, and in a backup copy that a grown-up saves (see “Backup copy”).

Reading aloud

When reading aloud is on, the app speaks the puzzle instructions with the speech synthesizer built into iOS on the device. No text or audio leaves the device, and the app never uses the microphone.

Motion sensor

Only while the result of a solved workshop is on the screen, the app reads the iPhone’s motion sensor on the device to notice playful moves that reveal hidden secrets, such as a gentle shake or turning the phone over. The readings are never stored or sent. The app does not use location, the camera, contacts or photos.

Third parties

The app shares no data with anyone: it contains no third-party code and talks to no servers. Apple handles downloads, updates and device backups under Apple’s own privacy policy.

If you chose in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and aggregated statistics that do not identify you.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays on your iPhone until you delete it. To remove one player’s progress, open Settings → Players, switch to another player and delete that one (a grown-up confirms it). The player who is playing now cannot be deleted. If a backup copy was restored earlier, the progress kept from before it may still include that player (see “Backup copy”). Deleting the app removes all players and all data from your device.

The app never sends us your data, so there is nothing from the app for us to keep, look up or delete.

Backup copy

In Settings → Backup copy, after passing the parental gate, a grown-up can save every player’s progress, the players’ names and the sound, haptics and read-aloud settings as one file. The file goes wherever the grown-up chooses in the Files window, for example on this iPhone or in iCloud Drive under Apple’s terms. The app never sends the file anywhere and keeps no copy of its own, and we never receive it.

Restoring a copy, also behind the parental gate, replaces all players and their progress on the device with the ones in the file. Deleting the app does not delete a saved copy: delete the file in Files when you no longer need it.

Before a restore replaces anything, the app keeps the progress that was on the device (all players, their names and the settings) in its own storage on this iPhone. A grown-up can then return it with Settings → Backup copy → Bring back the previous progress, behind the parental gate. Only one such state is kept, and a later restore can replace it. To tell whether anyone has played since a restore, the app also remembers what the restored file wrote. Both stay on the device and are never sent anywhere; deleting the app removes them. The kept progress does not change later: if a player is deleted, they stay in it, and bringing it back returns every player it holds.

Messages to support

If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and anything you include (for example, a screenshot). We use it only to answer you, share it with no one, and delete the correspondence within 12 months after the question is resolved, or sooner on request. E-mail is delivered by Apple’s iCloud Mail. Please ask a grown-up to write to us, not a child.

This website

This website uses no cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors’ IP addresses for security under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Children

The game is made for children and families. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. There are no ads, no purchases for real money, no accounts, no chat and no social media links. The ant shop uses only stars earned in the game.

Pages outside the game, such as this policy and the support page, open only after a grown-up passes a parental gate, and so do saving and restoring a backup copy. Because the app collects no personal information, there is no data collection for a parent to consent to.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) give you the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. The app gives us none; the only data we may hold is your messages to support. To ask about it or to have it deleted, write to exceptiongames@icloud.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
