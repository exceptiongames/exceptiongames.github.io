SupportLiving Factory

Living Factory is an ant-colony logic puzzle game for iPhone by exception games.

Contact

Write to exceptiongames@icloud.com. We read every message and answer in English, Ukrainian or Russian.

Reporting a bug? Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, the workshop number and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Questions and answers

Where is my progress saved?
Only on your iPhone. The game has no accounts and no cloud sync. Deleting the app erases all progress, so update the app without deleting it.
How do I start a workshop over?
Tap Restart. To replay the tutorial, open Settings → Replay tutorial.
I’m stuck. What can I do?
Tap Undo to take back the last signal, or tap Hint to see the next signal. A workshop solved with a hint earns one star; tap Restart and solve it on your own for more. The workshop of the day has no hint.
Can several people play on one iPhone?
Yes. Add players in Settings → Players. Each player has their own stars, records and ants.
How do I keep progress safe or move it to another iPhone?
Ask a grown-up to open Settings → Backup copy → Save a copy and keep the file in Files, for example in iCloud Drive. On the other iPhone, or if the game ever starts from scratch, use Restore from a copy. Restoring replaces the progress on that iPhone. If a grown-up picked the wrong file, Bring back the previous progress in the same place returns what was there before.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. There are no ads and no purchases for real money. Ants in the shop are bought with stars earned in the game.
Does the game collect data?
No. There are no analytics, accounts or tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
Which devices are supported?
iPhone with iOS 18 or later. The game works without the internet.
